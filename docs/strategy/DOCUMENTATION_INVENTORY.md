## Classification Definitions

- AUTHORITATIVE: current controlling source for a defined area.
- SUPPORTING: useful and generally current but not controlling.
- NEEDS REVISION: important but outdated, incomplete, or partially conflicting.
- HISTORICAL: valuable record of earlier decisions, completed phases, or product evolution.
- SUPERSEDED: replaced by newer direction but retained until archival cleanup.
- DUPLICATE: substantially repeats another document without meaningful unique content.
- TEMPORARY: one-time audit, scratchpad, handoff, generated report, or short-lived working document.
- UNREVIEWABLE: content could not be reliably inspected.
